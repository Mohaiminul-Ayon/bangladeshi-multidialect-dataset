Dataset methodology

A Human-Verified Multilingual & Multidialectal Dataset

A carefully curated language resource grounded in authentic Chittagonian media and extended through translation, dialect adaptation, domain annotation, and repeated human review.

Purpose

This dataset supports research and development for language technologies across the language and dialect labels recorded in the source data. It preserves locally grounded expressions while providing aligned Standard Bangla and English translations.

The collection is designed to reflect real spoken and broadcast language rather than isolated, artificial examples.

Core sources

Authentic Chittagonian songs and news content from Cplus TV were used as the initial data sources.

Language and dialect names should follow the exact labels used in the final dataset.

The workbook has 12 populated fields per record, including translations, domain, sentence type, verification status, difficulty, and notes. All 1,062 Pair IDs are unique; no cells are blank.

What makes this dataset novel

Its defining strength is a layered, community-centred validation process. AI supports refinement and verification, but local speakers and subject specialists make the final linguistic judgements.

  • Built from authentic regional media, including songs and news broadcasts.
  • Combines multilingual translation with multidialectal adaptation.
  • Uses local native speakers and experts to check meaning, wording, and dialect accuracy.
  • Includes domain detection and row-by-row, column-by-column verification.

Creation workflow

Source collectionGather Chittagonian songs and news content from Cplus TV.
TranscriptionCreate transcripts from the source videos.
AI refinementRefine the initial transcript with ChatGPT.
Local correctionNative local speakers manually correct sentences.
Specialist reviewSpecialists check linguistic quality and correctness.
TranslationTranslate approved content into Standard Bangla and English.
Dialect adaptationAdapt the content into additional local dialects represented in the dataset.
Expert verificationLocal speakers and experts manually review dialect translations.
Domain annotationWrite down and identify the domain for each item.
Final auditManually verify every row and column, with AI as a supporting check.

Quality assurance

Human-led accuracyLocal speakers correct linguistic details that automated systems may miss.
Expert-reviewed meaningSpecialist review adds an independent layer of linguistic assurance.
Full-record checkingEach row and column receives manual verification, supported by AI checks.
